About

Researcher who also ships

I'm a PhD candidate in Language and Literacies Education at OISE, University of Toronto, working at the intersection of multilingualism, mathematics education and human-AI interaction. My research asks a practical question: when a newcomer student opens an AI tool to do math homework, what has to be true for that tool to actually help?

I answer it with mixed methods — longitudinal classroom observation, interviews in Mandarin and English, usability testing, survey and platform analytics, and structured content analysis of AI outputs — and I close the loop by building. I designed and developed LinguaMath end-to-end, and I've contributed shipped learning-path features, hint systems and 34-language i18n to Binogi's production codebase.

Before the PhD I completed an M.Ed. in Curriculum Studies at OISE and a B.A. in English at Tianjin University. I'm a native Mandarin speaker, which is why my studies can read Chinese-language data directly rather than through translation.
